Support

One Pixel Diary is a private visual diary where one color becomes one mark in a growing story.

My purchase is not showing

Open One Pixel Diary, open the One Pixel+ screen and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

I changed or reinstalled my phone

One Pixel Diary does not use an account or cloud diary backend in version 1.0. Restore your diary from an encrypted .opdbackup file that you previously exported.

I forgot my backup password

Encrypted backups cannot be decrypted without the password used to create them. The password is not sent to or stored by the developer.

A restore says recovery is required

Do not continue editing. Follow the recovery screen in the app and keep all existing safety/exported backups until recovery completes successfully.

Notifications are not arriving

Check notification permission for One Pixel Diary in iOS Settings and confirm reminders are enabled inside the app.

Widget is not updating

Open One Pixel Diary once, record or edit an entry, then allow WidgetKit a moment to refresh. If necessary, remove and re-add the widget.

Privacy

One Pixel Diary has no advertising, no tracking and no developer-hosted diary backend. See the Privacy Policy.
