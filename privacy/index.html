Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

One Pixel Diary is designed as a private, local-first visual diary. The app does not require an account and does not operate a backend for diary content.

Information you create

Your Journeys, daily colors, patterns, notes, titles, settings and other diary content are stored locally on your device. One Pixel Diary does not send this diary content to the developer.

Purchases

One Pixel+ is a lifetime in-app purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment and purchase-account information. The app stores only a minimal local entitlement cache needed to keep a previously verified purchase available offline. The developer does not receive your diary content through StoreKit.

Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification text is generic and does not contain your diary notes, Journey title or selected colors.

Widget and Lock Screen

The widget reads a compact snapshot stored in the app's shared App Group container. The snapshot contains only the information needed to render the widget and excludes notes, personal motto, Journey title, backup data and purchase information.

Backups and exports

Encrypted diary backups are created on your device using a password you choose. A backup leaves the app only when you explicitly save or share it through Apple's system interfaces.

Artwork exports are also user-initiated. Notes and personal mottos are not included in exported artwork. Optional title/date presentation follows the choices shown in the export flow.

During a recovery operation, the app may retain an internal quarantine copy of the previous local diary bytes to reduce the risk of irreversible data loss. These recovery artifacts remain on the device and are not transmitted to the developer.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

One Pixel Diary contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or cross-app tracking. The developer does not sell personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Diary data stays on your device until you change or remove it, restore another backup through the app's guarded recovery flow, or delete the app. Internal safety backups and recovery artifacts are not uploaded to the developer.

Deleting the app removes data stored only inside its app container unless you previously exported a backup elsewhere.

Apple services

The app uses Apple platform services including StoreKit, WidgetKit and local notifications. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds data collection, online sync, analytics or other network services, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that behavior ships.
